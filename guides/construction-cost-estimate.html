How to Prepare a Preliminary House Construction Cost Estimate

Build a first-pass cost plan using built-up area, specification level, inclusions, exclusions and a contingency approach.

Step-by-step method

Start with a clearly defined built-up area and a rate basis that matches the intended scope. Separate structural work, masonry, plaster, flooring, doors and windows, electrical, plumbing, painting, external works, approvals and professional fees where applicable. Record what is included in every quotation and compare like-for-like specifications.

Worked example

If a preliminary rate is ₹2,000 per sq ft and built-up area is 1,200 sq ft, the simple area-rate multiplication is ₹24 lakh. It is only a broad starting point until the scope and specifications are defined.

Important checks and limitations

A single rate per square foot can hide major differences in soil, foundation, floor count, finish, services, location, taxes and contractor scope. Obtain measured BOQs and written quotations before committing funds.

Practical site tip

Keep a written record of the dimensions, units, material source, specification revision, assumptions and date of the estimate. This makes it easier to review differences between an early estimate, supplier quotation and actual site consumption.

Use the applicable project drawings, contract documents and local standards as the authority. Where a decision affects structural safety, ask the responsible qualified professional to verify it.
