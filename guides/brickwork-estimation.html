Brickwork Quantity Estimation: Bricks and Mortar

Learn how wall dimensions, brick module size, mortar joints, openings and breakage affect brickwork quantities.

Step-by-step method

Calculate gross wall volume from length × height × thickness. Estimate brick count using the actual brick module including mortar joints, or calculate by face area for a consistent wall thickness. Deduct door and window openings where the measurement method requires it. Estimate mortar from the difference between wall volume and the volume occupied by bricks, then apply an appropriate dry-volume allowance for the specified mix.

Worked example

A 3 m × 3 m wall with 0.23 m thickness has a gross volume of 2.07 m³ before opening deductions. A reliable brick count requires the measured brick and joint dimensions, not only the nominal label.

Important checks and limitations

Brick sizes vary between manufacturers and regions. Bond, joint thickness, breakage, wall openings and workmanship influence the result. Measure sample bricks and follow the BOQ measurement rules.

Practical site tip

Keep a written record of the dimensions, units, material source, specification revision, assumptions and date of the estimate. This makes it easier to review differences between an early estimate, supplier quotation and actual site consumption.

Use the applicable project drawings, contract documents and local standards as the authority. Where a decision affects structural safety, ask the responsible qualified professional to verify it.
