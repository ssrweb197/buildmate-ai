RCC Work Site Checklist Before Concrete Pouring

Organise pre-pour checks for formwork, reinforcement, cover, embedded services, access, concrete supply and inspection records.

Step-by-step method

Before a pour, verify the latest approved drawings and pour location. Check formwork dimensions, line, level, stability and cleanliness. Inspect reinforcement size, spacing, laps, anchorage, cover blocks and congestion. Confirm sleeves and embedded items, access for placing and vibration, pour sequence, concrete grade, delivery arrangements and testing requirements.

Worked example

A pre-pour checklist can record element ID, drawing revision, reinforcement inspection, formwork inspection, embedded services, concrete grade, slump or workability checks as specified, test samples and approval signatures.

Important checks and limitations

This is a general planning checklist. The project engineer, inspection and test plan, approved method statement and applicable codes take precedence. Do not alter reinforcement or concrete specifications without approval.

Practical site tip

Keep a written record of the dimensions, units, material source, specification revision, assumptions and date of the estimate. This makes it easier to review differences between an early estimate, supplier quotation and actual site consumption.

Use the applicable project drawings, contract documents and local standards as the authority. Where a decision affects structural safety, ask the responsible qualified professional to verify it.
