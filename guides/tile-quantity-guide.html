How to Estimate Floor Tile Quantity and Wastage

Estimate tile count from room area and tile dimensions, then plan for cuts, patterns, spare tiles and packaging.

Step-by-step method

Measure each room's usable floor area and calculate tile face area from the actual tile dimensions. Divide room area by tile area and round up to whole tiles or boxes. Add a project-appropriate allowance for cuts and breakage, with extra consideration for diagonal layouts, patterns, small rooms and future replacement stock.

Worked example

A 600 mm × 600 mm tile covers 0.36 m². A 20 m² floor needs at least 20/0.36 = 55.56, so 56 tiles before any cutting or breakage allowance.

Important checks and limitations

Do not rely on one allowance for every layout. Confirm box coverage, batch/shade consistency, joint width, skirting and spare-stock needs with the installer and supplier.

Practical site tip

Keep a written record of the dimensions, units, material source, specification revision, assumptions and date of the estimate. This makes it easier to review differences between an early estimate, supplier quotation and actual site consumption.

Use the applicable project drawings, contract documents and local standards as the authority. Where a decision affects structural safety, ask the responsible qualified professional to verify it.
