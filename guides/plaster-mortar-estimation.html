Plastering Mortar Calculation: Area, Thickness and Mix

Calculate wet plaster mortar volume and understand why dry-volume factors and surface condition affect material quantities.

Step-by-step method

Multiply plaster area in square metres by thickness converted from millimetres to metres. This gives wet mortar volume. Apply an appropriate estimating allowance for dry ingredients and divide by the selected cement:sand ratio. Check whether the specified system is conventional cement plaster, premix plaster or another product.

Worked example

For 100 m² at 12 mm, wet mortar volume is 100 × 0.012 = 1.20 m³. Any cement and sand estimate must then account for the specified mix and the chosen dry-volume convention.

Important checks and limitations

Uneven masonry, surface preparation, number of coats, mesh, sand moisture and product type change consumption. Use the manufacturer's coverage data for premix products.

Practical site tip

Keep a written record of the dimensions, units, material source, specification revision, assumptions and date of the estimate. This makes it easier to review differences between an early estimate, supplier quotation and actual site consumption.

Use the applicable project drawings, contract documents and local standards as the authority. Where a decision affects structural safety, ask the responsible qualified professional to verify it.
