How to Estimate Paint Quantity for Walls

Calculate paintable surface area and estimate litres using the product's stated coverage and number of coats.

Step-by-step method

Measure wall lengths and heights, add the areas, and deduct significant openings according to the level of accuracy required. Multiply the area by the number of coats and divide by the manufacturer's coverage per litre. Consider primer separately using its own coverage rate.

Worked example

For 100 m² of paintable surface, two coats and stated coverage of 10 m²/L per coat, the basic estimate is 100 × 2 / 10 = 20 litres before any allowance.

Important checks and limitations

Coverage varies with substrate, porosity, colour change, application method, dilution and product. Use the product data sheet and painter's site assessment.

Practical site tip

Keep a written record of the dimensions, units, material source, specification revision, assumptions and date of the estimate. This makes it easier to review differences between an early estimate, supplier quotation and actual site consumption.

Use the applicable project drawings, contract documents and local standards as the authority. Where a decision affects structural safety, ask the responsible qualified professional to verify it.
