M-Sand vs River Sand: What to Check Before Buying

Compare manufactured sand and natural river sand using grading, cleanliness, source quality, suitability and supplier documentation.

Step-by-step method

Do not choose sand by appearance or price alone. Ask for source details, grading information, silt or fines content, moisture condition and test results relevant to the intended use. Check the project's specification and confirm suitability with the engineer or concrete supplier.

Worked example

For concrete, masonry mortar and plaster, the grading and fines requirements may differ. A material suitable for one application is not automatically suitable for another.

Important checks and limitations

Local specifications and standards govern acceptance. This guide is a purchasing checklist, not a declaration that a particular sand source meets a standard.

Practical site tip

Keep a written record of the dimensions, units, material source, specification revision, assumptions and date of the estimate. This makes it easier to review differences between an early estimate, supplier quotation and actual site consumption.

Use the applicable project drawings, contract documents and local standards as the authority. Where a decision affects structural safety, ask the responsible qualified professional to verify it.
