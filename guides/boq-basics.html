BOQ Basics: Quantity, Unit, Rate and Amount

Understand the structure of a bill of quantities and how to make line items measurable and comparable.

Step-by-step method

A BOQ line normally identifies the work description, unit of measurement, quantity, rate and amount. Define scope and measurement rules clearly. Keep units consistent, avoid duplicate scope and state assumptions, exclusions and specification references. Multiply quantity by rate for each line, then total the lines and show taxes or other adjustments separately as required.

Worked example

A line for 25 m² at ₹450/m² gives ₹11,250. A useful BOQ also explains exactly which material, workmanship, preparation and finishing the rate includes.

Important checks and limitations

Contract documents and applicable standard measurement methods determine the correct BOQ structure. A quick quantity-times-rate tool is not a substitute for a measured project BOQ.

Practical site tip

Keep a written record of the dimensions, units, material source, specification revision, assumptions and date of the estimate. This makes it easier to review differences between an early estimate, supplier quotation and actual site consumption.

Use the applicable project drawings, contract documents and local standards as the authority. Where a decision affects structural safety, ask the responsible qualified professional to verify it.
