RCC Material Calculator

Estimate cement, sand and aggregate for preliminary nominal-mix concrete quantities.

Open RCC Material Calculator →

How this calculator works

Enter length, width and depth in metres, then choose the nominal mix grade. The calculator first finds wet concrete volume and applies an assumed dry-volume factor of 1.54. It divides the dry volume according to the selected nominal proportion and converts cement volume to approximate 50 kg bags using 0.0347 m³ per bag.

Worked example

For a 3 m × 3 m × 0.15 m slab, wet concrete volume is 1.35 m³. For a nominal 1:1.5:3 mix, the estimated dry material volume is 2.079 m³ before splitting into cement, sand and aggregate. The result is a preliminary material estimate, not a mix design.

Important assumptions and limitations

M25 and higher grades require approved design-mix proportions. Nominal proportions are not a substitute for project specifications, trial mixes or professional approval.

How to get a reliable estimate

Use measured dimensions and consistent units. Confirm the applicable drawings, material specifications, product data sheets and local construction requirements. Review the result and assumptions before using it for budgeting or procurement. For safety-critical or structural decisions, consult a qualified professional and use approved project calculations.

This tool is provided for preliminary planning and educational use. It is not a certified design, quotation or substitute for site verification.
