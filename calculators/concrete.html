How this calculator works

Enter length, width and depth in metres. The calculator multiplies these dimensions to find wet concrete volume and also shows a dry-material planning allowance using a 1.54 factor.

Worked example

A 3 m × 3 m × 0.15 m slab has a geometric volume of 1.35 m³.

Important assumptions and limitations

The dry-volume factor is only a rough estimating convention. Concrete ordering should account for the actual mix, formwork, placement method, wastage and project specification.

How to get a reliable estimate

Use measured dimensions and consistent units. Confirm the applicable drawings, material specifications, product data sheets and local construction requirements. Review the result and assumptions before using it for budgeting or procurement. For safety-critical or structural decisions, consult a qualified professional and use approved project calculations.

This tool is provided for preliminary planning and educational use. It is not a certified design, quotation or substitute for site verification.
