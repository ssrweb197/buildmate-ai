Footing Concrete Calculator

Calculate preliminary concrete volume and nominal-mix material quantities for a footing.

Open Footing Concrete Calculator →

How this calculator works

Enter the footing length, width and depth in metres. The calculator treats the footing as a rectangular prism and estimates concrete volume and nominal-mix materials.

Worked example

A 1.8 m × 1.8 m × 0.45 m rectangular footing has a geometric concrete volume of 1.458 m³ before any deductions or allowances.

Important assumptions and limitations

Footing dimensions, reinforcement, founding level and concrete grade must come from approved structural design and site conditions. This tool does not design a footing.

How to get a reliable estimate

Use measured dimensions and consistent units. Confirm the applicable drawings, material specifications, product data sheets and local construction requirements. Review the result and assumptions before using it for budgeting or procurement. For safety-critical or structural decisions, consult a qualified professional and use approved project calculations.

This tool is provided for preliminary planning and educational use. It is not a certified design, quotation or substitute for site verification.
