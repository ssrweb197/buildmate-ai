Brickwork Calculator

Estimate traditional brick counts and mortar materials from wall dimensions.

Open Brickwork Calculator →

How this calculator works

Enter wall length, height and thickness in metres and choose a mortar ratio. The current calculator assumes a traditional brick size of 230 × 110 × 75 mm and a nominal module of 240 × 120 × 85 mm including joints. It adds a 5% allowance to the calculated brick count and estimates mortar from the assumed brick volume.

Worked example

A 3 m long, 3 m high, 0.23 m thick wall has a gross volume of 2.07 m³. The number of bricks depends strongly on the actual brick dimensions and mortar-joint thickness, so measure a sample batch before ordering.

Important assumptions and limitations

This tool does not deduct openings such as doors and windows. Actual brick dimensions, bond, joint thickness, breakage and workmanship change the result.

How to get a reliable estimate

Use measured dimensions and consistent units. Confirm the applicable drawings, material specifications, product data sheets and local construction requirements. Review the result and assumptions before using it for budgeting or procurement. For safety-critical or structural decisions, consult a qualified professional and use approved project calculations.

This tool is provided for preliminary planning and educational use. It is not a certified design, quotation or substitute for site verification.
