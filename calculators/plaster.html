Plastering Quantity Calculator

Estimate cement and sand for plastering from surface area and thickness.

How this calculator works

Enter plaster area in square metres and thickness in millimetres. The calculator converts thickness to metres, finds wet mortar volume, applies an assumed dry-volume factor of 1.33 and splits the dry volume using the selected cement:sand ratio.

Worked example

For 100 m² at 12 mm thickness, wet mortar volume is 1.20 m³. The dry-volume allowance is about 1.596 m³ before splitting the selected ratio.

Important assumptions and limitations

Actual consumption depends on surface unevenness, background preparation, sand grading, mortar type, number of coats and wastage. Use the specified plaster mix.

How to get a reliable estimate

Use measured dimensions and consistent units. Confirm the applicable drawings, material specifications, product data sheets and local construction requirements. Review the result and assumptions before using it for budgeting or procurement. For safety-critical or structural decisions, consult a qualified professional and use approved project calculations.

This tool is provided for preliminary planning and educational use. It is not a certified design, quotation or substitute for site verification.
