How this calculator works

Enter a positive quantity and a rate in matching units. The calculator multiplies them to return one line amount. It is a quick arithmetic helper, not a full BOQ with measurement rules, descriptions, specifications or tax calculations.

Worked example

A quantity of 25 m² at ₹450 per m² gives a line amount of ₹11,250.

Important assumptions and limitations

Check the unit of measurement and rate basis. Taxes, labour, overheads, wastage, escalation and other contract items may need separate lines.

How to get a reliable estimate

Use measured dimensions and consistent units. Confirm drawings, material specifications, product data sheets and local construction requirements. Review the result and assumptions before using it for budgeting or procurement. For structural decisions, consult a qualified professional and use approved project calculations.

This tool is for preliminary planning and educational use. It is not a certified design or quotation.
