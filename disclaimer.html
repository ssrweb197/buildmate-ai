Engineering Disclaimer

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Preliminary estimates only

BuildMate AI calculators provide general educational and preliminary quantity-planning estimates. Outputs depend on the dimensions, units, options and assumptions entered by the user. They may not include every project-specific factor, opening, lap, bend, wastage, compaction, site condition or specification.

No structural design advice

These tools do not design or certify foundations, columns, beams, slabs, reinforcement, retaining structures or other structural elements. Do not use calculator outputs as the sole basis for construction, procurement or safety-critical decisions. Obtain project-specific calculations and approvals from qualified professionals.

Mix proportions and material quantities

Nominal mix calculations are simplified estimates and are not approved concrete mix designs. Where design mix is required, use the approved mix design and project specifications. Verify material quantities, product coverage and construction methods before ordering or execution.

Rates and costs

Indicative prices and cost estimates are not offers or quotations. Market prices change by supplier, brand, grade, location, quantity, transport, taxes and date. Obtain current written quotations.

Use at your own judgement

Users are responsible for checking inputs, units, outputs and local requirements. If you identify an error, please contact us so it can be reviewed.
