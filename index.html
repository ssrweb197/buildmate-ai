ABOUT BUILDMATE AI

Practical tools for everyday construction planning

Construction work involves repeated calculations: concrete volume, cement bags, sand and aggregate quantities, brick counts, plaster mortar, reinforcement weight, floor area and preliminary project costs. BuildMate AI is designed to make these routine calculations quicker to estimate and easier to review.

The calculator collection includes tools for RCC and PCC concrete, brickwork, blockwork, plastering, mortar, footing, columns, beams, slabs, flooring, paint, excavation, steel weight, staircase geometry, slope, unit weight, area and volume, and a basic BOQ estimate. Enter the dimensions and other requested inputs to see an indicative result. Where a tool uses assumptions—such as a nominal mix ratio, dry-volume factor, wastage allowance or material coverage—review the note shown with the result before using it.

These tools are intended for preliminary quantity planning, learning and quick cross-checks. They do not replace approved structural drawings, project specifications, laboratory test results, an approved concrete mix design, site measurements or professional engineering judgement. Actual consumption and costs depend on workmanship, material quality, local rates, transport, taxes, site conditions and project requirements.

Students can use the calculators to understand common quantity relationships. Homeowners and site teams can use them to prepare early estimates and questions for contractors or suppliers. Always compare estimates with current supplier quotations and have important quantities and structural decisions checked by a qualified professional.

How to use the calculators

Choose a calculator, enter dimensions in the units displayed beside each field, select a mix or ratio where applicable, and review the result and its assumptions. Use consistent units throughout each calculation. If a result appears unusual, recheck the dimensions and selected options rather than relying on the number automatically.

Engineering and safety note

Calculator results are preliminary estimates. Structural design, reinforcement detailing, foundation sizing, concrete grades, mix proportions and code compliance must be determined from project-specific information and verified by qualified professionals under applicable standards. Never use a simple quantity calculator as the sole basis for structural construction decisions.
